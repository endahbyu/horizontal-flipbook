MCMC Plan 2026–2030 Advancing Inclusivity Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission

Open Flipbook

By 2030, Malaysia’s communications and multimedia landscape will be more intelligent, interconnected and complex. Artificial intelligence, advanced connectivity, data-driven services, converging platforms and emerging technologies will continue to reshape how people communicate, how businesses create value and how public services are delivered. At the same time, expectations for trust, safety, inclusion, resilience and regulatory responsiveness will continue to rise.

MCMC Plan 2026–2030 Action Plan Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission

Open Flipbook

The Action Plan establishes the five-year strategic intent, 2030 outcomes, indicative measures, priority interventions, delivery ownership and implementation horizons. Implementation will be operationalised through MCMC’s Annual Work Plan (AWP), which will translate the multi-year portfolio into annual programmes and initiatives, KPIs, baselines, numerical targets, milestones, resource requirements and accountable delivery commitments.
